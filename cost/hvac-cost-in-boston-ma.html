How much does HVAC Repair cost in Boston?

Expect roughly $143 per hour in Boston, with most work landing between $124 and $234 per hour. A typical minimum service call is about $169. The table above shows real-world ranges for common hvac jobs.

What is the average hourly rate for hvac repair in Boston?

The local average is about $143 per hour, which is 30% above the national average of $110. Rates vary by company size, licensing, insurance and how quickly you need someone on site.

Do hvac prices go up for emergency or after-hours work?

Yes. Emergency, weekend and overnight calls commonly add 25% to 50% on top of standard rates in Boston. If the job can safely wait until normal business hours, you will usually save money.

Do I need a permit for this kind of work in MA?

Massachusetts requires a state Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration and separate trade licenses for plumbing and electrical work. Permits, where required, usually add $78 to $455 to the total.

How can I keep hvac costs down in Boston?

Get at least three written quotes, bundle small tasks into one visit to avoid repeat service charges, ask whether the diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair, and schedule non-urgent work outside peak season. Always confirm the contractor is licensed and insured before work begins.
