What affects the price

  • Size of the affected area
  • Where the mold is - attics and crawlspaces cost more
  • Whether the moisture source has been fixed
  • Whether containment and negative air machines are needed
  • Emergency, weekend or after-hours scheduling

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mold Remediation cost in Colorado Springs?

Expect roughly $100 per hour in Colorado Springs, with most work landing between $85 and $160 per hour. A typical minimum service call is about $250. The table above shows real-world ranges for common mold removal jobs.

What is the average hourly rate for mold remediation in Colorado Springs?

The local average is about $100 per hour, which is right at the national average. Rates vary by company size, licensing, insurance and how quickly you need someone on site.

Do mold removal prices go up for emergency or after-hours work?

Yes. Emergency, weekend and overnight calls commonly add 25% to 50% on top of standard rates in Colorado Springs. If the job can safely wait until normal business hours, you will usually save money.

Do I need a permit for this kind of work in CO?

Colorado does not license most contractors statewide; licensing and permits are handled by the city or county, and Denver has its own rules. Permits, where required, usually add $60 to $350 to the total.

How can I keep mold removal costs down in Colorado Springs?

Get at least three written quotes, bundle small tasks into one visit to avoid repeat service charges, ask whether the diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair, and schedule non-urgent work outside peak season. Always confirm the contractor is licensed and insured before work begins.
