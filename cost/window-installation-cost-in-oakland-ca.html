What affects the price

  • Window material: vinyl, wood or fiberglass
  • Whether the full frame is replaced or an insert is used
  • Glass type - impact or energy-rated glass costs more
  • Number of windows and access from outside
  • Emergency, weekend or after-hours scheduling
  • Permit and inspection fees where required
  • Access, age of the home and condition of existing systems

Licensing and permits in CA

California requires most contractors to hold a state license from the CSLB. Check any license number at cslb.ca.gov, and confirm permits for structural, electrical, plumbing or roofing work.
