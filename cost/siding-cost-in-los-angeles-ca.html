What affects the price

  • Material choice: vinyl, fiber-cement or wood
  • Whether old siding must be removed first
  • Square footage and number of stories
  • Trim, soffit and fascia work included
  • Emergency, weekend or after-hours scheduling
  • Permit and inspection fees where required
  • Access, age of the home and condition of existing systems

Licensing and permits in CA

California requires most contractors to hold a state license from the CSLB. Check any license number at cslb.ca.gov, and confirm permits for structural, electrical, plumbing or roofing work.

What is the average hourly rate for siding installation in Los Angeles?

The local average is about $12 per hour, which is 30% above the national average of $9. Rates vary by company size, licensing, insurance and how quickly you need someone on site.

Do siding prices go up for emergency or after-hours work?

Yes. Emergency, weekend and overnight calls commonly add 25% to 50% on top of standard rates in Los Angeles. If the job can safely wait until normal business hours, you will usually save money.

Do I need a permit for this kind of work in CA?

California requires most contractors to hold a state license from the CSLB. Check any license number at cslb.ca.gov, and confirm permits for structural, electrical, plumbing or roofing work. Permits, where required, usually add $78 to $455 to the total.

How can I keep siding costs down in Los Angeles?

Get at least three written quotes, bundle small tasks into one visit to avoid repeat service charges, ask whether the diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair, and schedule non-urgent work outside peak season. Always confirm the contractor is licensed and insured before work begins.
