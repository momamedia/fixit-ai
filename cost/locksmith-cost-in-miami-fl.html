Frequently asked questions

How much does a Locksmith cost in Miami?

Expect roughly $94 per hour in Miami, with most work landing between $77 and $154 per hour. A typical minimum service call is about $99. The table above shows real-world ranges for common locksmith jobs.

What is the average hourly rate for locksmith service in Miami?

The local average is about $94 per hour, which is 10% above the national average of $85. Rates vary by company size, licensing, insurance and how quickly you need someone on site.

Do locksmith prices go up for emergency or after-hours work?

Yes. Emergency, weekend and overnight calls commonly add 25% to 50% on top of standard rates in Miami. If the job can safely wait until normal business hours, you will usually save money.

Do I need a permit for this kind of work in FL?

Florida licenses contractors through the DBPR and enforces some of the strictest hurricane and building codes in the country, especially for roofing and windows. Permits, where required, usually add $66 to $385 to the total.

How can I keep locksmith costs down in Miami?

Get at least three written quotes, bundle small tasks into one visit to avoid repeat service charges, ask whether the diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair, and schedule non-urgent work outside peak season. Always confirm the contractor is licensed and insured before work begins.
