Do garage door prices go up for emergency or after-hours work?

Yes. Emergency, weekend and overnight calls commonly add 25% to 50% on top of standard rates in Boston. If the job can safely wait until normal business hours, you will usually save money.

How can I keep garage door costs down in Boston?
