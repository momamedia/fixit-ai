Tools & supplies you may need

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using the wrong tools or materials, which can worsen gutters problems or void warranties.

When to call a professional

Some gutters problems are signs of a bigger issue. Call a qualified pro if you notice persistent leaks, water damage, burning or gas smells, sparking or exposed wiring, structural concerns, or if the fix requires permits or specialized equipment. A pro can diagnose the root cause and help you avoid repeat repairs.
