Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Top Shield Home Services collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, chat with our assistant, or request help with home services.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Top Shield Home Services ("we," "us," or "our") helps connect homeowners with home service professionals. By using our website or chat, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Contact and service details you share in chat or requests, such as your first name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, state, and a description of the issue you need help with.
  • Chat messages and related details you send to our AI assistant so we can understand your request and connect you with the right service.
  • Basic usage information that websites typically receive, such as browser type, device information, pages viewed, and approximate location, which may be collected through logs or similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To understand your request and match you with an appropriate home service or professional.
  • To contact you or help a service provider contact you about the request you submitted.
  • To operate, improve, and secure our website, chat experience, and services.
  • To respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the service you asked for, including:

  • Service providers and partners: we may share your request details and contact information with independent home service professionals, contractors, networks, or partners so they can respond to your request or connect with you.
  • Service vendors: we may use vendors to help run our website and communications (for example, hosting, communications, or technology providers), who process information on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the security of our services.

We do not sell your personal information in the ordinary sense of exchanging it for money. How third parties handle information they receive may be governed by their own policies.

AI assistant

Our site includes an AI-powered chat to help identify your needs and collect details to route your request. AI responses may be imperfect — please confirm important details. Messages you send are used to provide and improve the chat experience and to fulfill your request.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our vendors may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality, performance, and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep information as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill your request, operate our services, resolve issues, and meet legal requirements. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, but we may be unable to connect you with a service without it. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to or deletion of certain personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
